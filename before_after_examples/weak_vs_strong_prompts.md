# Weak vs Strong Prompts

## Example 1

Weak:
Make this better.

Strong:
Rewrite the following message in a professional tone for an IT support ticket update. Keep it under 80 words and do not add new information.

## Example 2

Weak:
Check this AI answer.

Strong:
Evaluate the AI answer for accuracy, completeness, clarity, instruction following, and hallucination risk. Score each category from 1 to 5 and explain your reasoning.

## Example 3

Weak:
Summarize this.

Strong:
Summarize the following technical incident for a non-technical manager. Include issue, impact, resolution, and next steps in four bullet points.
